How long does a menu take?

3 to 7 days. Once you send us your menu items and prices, we deliver the initial preview within 3 days and finalize delivery shortly after your review.

What do I need to send you?

Menu photo or text, logo, and any dish photos you would like to include.

Can I change prices myself later?

Yes, we show you how, or send us the change over WhatsApp and we will update it for you.

Are there monthly fees?

Our digital menu sites run on reliable, cost-effective static cloud hosting. Contact us and we will explain the hosting and domain options clearly with zero recurring platform lock-in.

What are the AI reels?

3 short promo videos made with AI tools for your restaurant, included free with a menu order. Formatted for Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and WhatsApp Status.

Do you work outside Coimbatore?

Yes, we work remotely with businesses across India over WhatsApp and video calls.
